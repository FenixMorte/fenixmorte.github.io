Hero Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Hero Ledger keeps your characters on your device. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. The only data that leaves your device is what Google collects to show ads and process purchases, described below.

Your characters and content

Characters, homebrew content and imported books are stored only on your device. We never receive them. When you export or share a character, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (based on your IP address), device information, and how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads use this data for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time under About > Privacy options in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your account owns the purchase, so it can turn ads off.

Children

Hero Ledger is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
